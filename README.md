## Strategy

Tide Hunter filters out the noise of pump.fun token launches and rides the strongest waves using a disciplined, momentum-based approach:

- **Selective Entry:** Only enters token launches that exhibit strong early buy volume and consistent positive flow on the bonding curve.
- **Initial Probe:** Buys a small position first to test the waters without taking on outsized risk.
- **Pyramiding:** Adds to the position incrementally as the momentum confirms and strength builds.
- **Trailing Stop:** Rides winning runners upward, protecting gains dynamically with a trailing stop-loss.
- **Strict Risk Control:** Cuts losers immediately at -15% against the average cost basis.
- **Position Cap:** Never exceeds 5 open positions simultaneously to maintain focus and capital efficiency.
